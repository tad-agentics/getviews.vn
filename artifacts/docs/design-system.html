01 · Foundations

Five layers, one opinion per layer.

Every Getviews surface is built from the same five foundational layers — color, type, spacing, shape, motion. Each one carries a single opinion. When in doubt, choose the smaller move: a tighter spacing step, a quieter weight, a calmer easing. The brand earns its boldness by being quiet everywhere else.

Eight steps, three weights, one italic.

06 · Motion

Fast, decisive, never bouncy.

Motion is feedback — never decoration. Three durations, two easings, one named animation per concept. Anything over 400ms is making the user wait; anything under 100ms is invisible. Bouncy springs and elastic curves are off-limits — Getviews is a tool, not a toy.

14 · Overlays

Modal is the nuclear option.

Prefer inline. Prefer a drawer. Prefer a popover. Reach for a modal only when the user must decide before continuing — discard unsaved changes, confirm a destructive action, complete onboarding. Tooltips explain, never decide. Toasts announce, never block.

16 · Voice in UI

Direct, specific, in your corner.

The product talks like a sharp editor who's already watched your last ten videos. Vietnamese, second-person bạn, no exclamation points, no emoji. Numbers before adjectives. The system says what happened, why it matters, and what to do next — in that order.

DO · button copy
Phân tích video này
Verb + object. Tells the user exactly what happens. No "Submit" or "Go" or "Start now!".
DON'T · vague verb
Bắt đầu →
Begin what? Tomorrow's lottery? Always name the action.
DO · empty state
Chưa có nháp nào. Dán link để bắt đầu.
Honest + actionable. One next step, not a sales pitch.
DON'T · marketing in product
🚀 Sẵn sàng tạo nội dung viral của bạn?
No emoji in product. No "viral". No exclamation. No "your content" hyperbole.
DO · error message
Link không hợp lệ — cần dạng tiktok.com/@handle
What's wrong + the exact shape we need. The user can fix it in 4 seconds.
DON'T · system-speak
Error 422: validation failed.
Codes are for logs. The user gets a sentence they can act on.
House style · the 9 rules
  • Always Vietnamese. Always bạn, never quý khách.
  • Numbers before adjectives. "4.2M views" not "a lot of views".
  • No exclamation points. The product is calm.
  • No emoji in product surfaces. Marketing only.
  • Buttons start with a verb. "Phân tích", "Lưu", "Kết nối".
  • Titles are sentence case. Only proper nouns capitalised.
  • Errors name the fix, not the failure.
  • Empty states give one action, not a list.
  • Time is human: "2 phút trước", not "120s ago".
17 · Rules to avoid

Things we will not do — ever.

A design system isn't just a list of approved moves — it's also a list of bad habits we've named so we can stop ourselves. Every rule below traces back to a real screen where the wrong choice almost shipped. Treat these as load-bearing; the brand falls apart in the small decisions, not the big ones.

A · Identity

Don't borrow from the platforms we sit next to.

Don't
tiktok pink + cyan
Never use TikTok or Douyin brand colors
We work for creators on TikTok, but we don't dress like TikTok. Our magenta is hotter; our sky is cleaner. Stock platform hues read as fan-art.
Don't
No gradients on the mark, the wordmark, or any brand asset
The mark is a single solid fill. Gradients turn it into a sticker. Magenta + sky belong on different elements, never blended.
Don't
No more than one magenta button per screen
Magenta is the moment of action. Three magenta buttons = three primary actions = no primary action. Ink fill for the rest.
B · Type

Three weights, one italic, sentence case.

Don't
Track every video that mattered today
Never italic across a whole headline or sentence
Instrument Serif italic is for one emotional word. Whole-sentence italic stops being a beat and starts being a wedding invitation.
Don't
Inter · Roboto · SF Pro
none of these belong here
Only three families exist
Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif, JetBrains Mono. Substituting Inter or system-ui breaks the metrics our scale is tuned to.
Don't
Title Case Everywhere
Never Title Case buttons or headings
Sentence case. "Phân tích video này" — not "Phân Tích Video Này". Title case is American agency speak; we don't.
C · Components

Cards, not chrome.

Don't
NEW FEATURE
Card with accent left-border
The most overused SaaS cliché of 2024.
No accent left-border + rounded card pattern
It's the generic AI-SaaS slop trope. If a card needs emphasis, use header weight or a chip — not a coloured edge.
Don't
Bạn có chắc không?
Click to dismiss
No modal for a non-blocking decision
If the user can keep working without answering, it's not a modal. Use an inline notice, a toast, or a drawer.
Don't
Triple-nested
Russian dolls
Never nest cards more than one level deep
Card-in-card-in-card is the death of hierarchy. One nested level max (radius drops from 18 → 12). After that, switch to a divider line.
D · Motion

Feedback, not theatre.

Don't
No bouncy or elastic easing
Springs, overshoot, wobble — not us. We use cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1) for everything that needs to feel decisive.
Don't
decorative bg motion
No ambient or decorative animation
