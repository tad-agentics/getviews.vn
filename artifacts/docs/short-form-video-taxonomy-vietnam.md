# Short-Form Video Creative Evaluation: A Comprehensive Scoring Taxonomy
## Vietnam Edition — Updated with Vietnamese TikTok Market Layer

> Global framework synthesized from agency research (VaynerMedia, Jungle Creations, Whalar), platform data (TikTok for Business, Meta, YouTube Creator Insider), OpusClip's 34,635-clip analysis, and Paddy Galloway's 3.3B-Short study. Vietnamese layer built from: EQVN, INS Media, Kind Content Academy practitioner frameworks; YouNet Media Slang Report 2025; Vietnamese TikTok market research (76.1M users, $3.4B TikTok Shop GMV H1 2025); and Vietnamese creator terminology corpus.

## §0. Commerce Intent Layer [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC — evaluate first]

This section has no Western equivalent. In Vietnamese TikTok, every creative decision is downstream of commerce intent. Evaluate this before anything else.

- **Conversion objective**: What is this video actually trying to do?
  - TikTok Shop direct sale (yellow cart / giỏ hàng vàng)
  - Shopee affiliate link (orange cart / giỏ hàng cam + bio link)
  - Livestream funnel (video is a trailer — drives viewers into a live session)
  - Brand deal soft-sell (disclosure required under Vietnam Ad Law 2025)
  - Follower/KOC growth (brand deal pipeline building — no direct conversion)
- **Product price tier**: Determines hook strategy, pacing, and trust requirements
  - Sub-150K VND: impulse purchase — shock/price-anchor hook, fast CTA
  - 150K–500K VND: considered purchase — demo + social proof required
  - 500K+ VND: high-consideration — authority hook, extended benefit explanation
- **Content type classification**:
  - KOC review ("mình dùng thật rồi mới review" — authentic consumer review)
  - KOS/seller direct (creator owns/sells the product — urgency-driven)
  - Brand deal (paid partnership — disclosure legally required)
  - Entertainment-first (commerce secondary — audience building)
  - Livestream entry point (hook drives into live session, not direct purchase)
- **Commerce CTA audit**: Verbal CTA must be spoken aloud (not just displayed as text) — this is a documented Vietnamese conversion best practice. Check for: "Bấm vào giỏ hàng vàng," "Link ở bio," "Mã giảm giá trong comment," arrow sticker pointing to cart icon.
- **Disclosure compliance (Vietnam Ad Law 2025)**: Brand deals must disclose commercial relationships. Non-disclosure carries fines of 20–30M VND (~$800–1,200). Flag: is the commercial relationship visible and disclosed?

## §1. Metadata & Platform Signals

- **Caption / on-video copy**: Native length (TikTok rewards 80–150 char captions with searchable keywords). TikTok OCR scans every text overlay for banned terms — verify against Vietnamese restricted phrase list (see §10). Check keyword density: does the same term appear in voice, OCR text, and caption simultaneously?
- **Hashtags**: 1–5 specific descriptive tags outperform #fyp spam. Audit: restricted/banned tag presence; niche-specific vs. broad-discovery mix. Vietnam: #tiepthilienket, #affiliatetiktok, #tiktokshopvn, #reviewdoshopee, #koc are the core commerce ecosystem tags. Niche-specific hashtags generate 30% higher engagement than generic ones.
- **Sound choice**: Original audio vs. licensed Commercial Music Library (mandatory for Business accounts). Mismatched usage strips audio. Vietnamese-specific: V-pop or Vietnamese remix trending sound in the first 24–48 hours of emergence is a known reach multiplier — assess sound trend lifecycle stage (see §8).
- **Douyin origin tag [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: Does this format, sound, or visual style trace to a Douyin precedent? If yes, what is the current Vietnam adoption curve stage? (Emerging / Growth / Peak / Parody / Decline — typical 2–4 week lag from Douyin to Vietnam)
- **Cover / thumbnail / first frame**: First frame is the de-facto thumbnail on Shorts and Reels. Audit: contrast, face presence (lifts CTR), visible text hook, legible at mobile size. For Vietnamese commerce content: does the first frame show the product, the result, or the creator's face reacting? Each signals a different hook archetype.
- **Video length**: TikTok 7–15s for hook tests; 21–34s for highest completion in most verticals. Vietnamese affiliate formula: 30–60s is the dominant commerce format (enough time for 5-phase structure). Sub-30s for entertainment hooks.
- **Aspect ratio & safe zones**: 9:16 at 1080×1920 only. Keep type/CTAs in the central 80% — top 15% / bottom 35% reserved for UI overlays. On Vietnamese TikTok Shop videos, the yellow cart icon occupies the right side — ensure no text overlaps it.
- **Account-level metadata**: Business vs. Personal (limits music library). Vietnamese context: Business accounts cannot use most trending V-pop sounds — this is a significant strategic constraint for commerce creators.
- **File hygiene**: No foreign-platform watermarks. Re-uploads detected by hash fingerprint.

## §2. Context

- **Niche fit / topical relevance**: TikTok's account profiling system. Videos that drift from the account's established niche graph see deprioritized FYP distribution. Vietnamese context: the 17 high-volume niches (skincare, food, fashion, fitness, home gadgets, MMO, etc.) have distinct hook and pacing norms.
- **Competitive context**: Benchmark against same-niche top-decile retention and saturation. Vietnamese commerce niches are high-velocity — top skincare videos are refreshed weekly; a format more than 3 weeks old may already be saturated.
- **Posting cadence & momentum**: Vietnamese education is uniquely fixated on "khung giờ vàng" (golden hours): 6–9 AM, 11:30 AM–1:30 PM, 6–8 PM, 10 PM–midnight. Thursday 7–9 PM = peak engagement. Sudden cadence breaks suppress momentum; post 10–20 min before peak hours for algorithm warm-up.
- **Account history / authority**: New accounts get smaller seed-audience tests. Vietnamese context: KOC accounts under 50K followers are treated as "người tiêu dùng thật" (real consumers) — their authenticity signals are more valuable than production polish.
- **Nhóm tương tác chéo flag [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: Institutionalized engagement exchange groups (Zalo, Messenger, TikTok group chats) systematically seed early likes/comments/views. A video's first-hour engagement spike may reflect cross-engagement group activity rather than organic algorithm response. Flag when early engagement velocity is high but watch-time metrics are weak.
- **Algorithmic surface**: FYP (cold-start), Following (warm), Search (intent), Hashtag (topic), Profile (returning). A 73% FYP-share account demands content scripted for someone who has never heard of you. Vietnamese commerce creators should target Search increasingly — TikTok VN is becoming a product discovery engine.

## §3. Hook Analysis (0–3 Seconds)

The single highest-leverage variable. OpusClip's 34,635-clip analysis found top-performing hook types average ~2× the views of bottom-ranked hooks. Vietnamese educators confirm: 55.4% of Vietnamese viewers will watch an entire video if hooked within the first 5 seconds.

### Global hook archetypes (auditable as discrete categories)

- **Product/outcome showcase** (highest performer in OpusClip data): lead with the finished result/transformation
- **Curiosity gap**: opens a loop the brain must close. Vietnamese: "Bạn có biết 80% người dùng TikTok đang mắc phải sai lầm này?"
- **Bold claim / contrarian statement**: requires payoff within 10–15s
- **Pattern interrupt**: unexpected visual/audio (zoom, crash, off-angle)
- **Question hook**: creates information void
- **Problem / pain-point**: "Tired of X?" — high in DTC/SaaS and Vietnamese commerce
- **Storytime / POV**: parasocial bond. Vietnamese: "Mình đã dùng qua 5 loại, đều thất vọng vì…"
- **Shock / controversy**: high reach, brand-equity risk. Note: controversy hooks perform better on YouTube Shorts than on TikTok Vietnam FYP — sound-driven hooks outperform controversy hooks on TikTok VN
- **Social proof / proof-first**: lead with the result or credential
- **Transformation / before-after**: visual delta in first frame. Vietnamese: "Trước và sau khi sử dụng…"
- **Listicle / number hook**: "5 reasons…" — high save rate. Vietnamese: "3 món đồ 'nhỏ mà có võ'"
- **Fear / FOMO**: loss-aversion lever. Vietnamese: "Bạn có muốn biết cách kiếm 10 triệu đồng mỗi tháng từ TikTok không?"
- **Direct address**: "You spend 4 hours a week on meals you don't even like"

### Vietnam-specific hook categories [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]

- **Vạch trần / Exposé hook**: "Đây là những thứ tui chấm 10/10 nhưng trên thị trường rất flop" — combines authority with revelation. Dominant in beauty/skincare.
- **Giá sốc / Price-anchor hook**: shows crossed-out original price vs. sale price in frame 1. Commerce-specific. "Giá gốc 500K — hôm nay chỉ 129K." Impulse trigger.
- **FOMO urgency hook**: "Flash sale / chỉ hôm nay / số lượng có hạn / bên em chỉ đi 10 đơn cuối." Dominant in TikTok Shop / livestream entry.
- **Dialect identity hook [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: Regional dialect used in opening line (Huế, Quảng Nam, Southern, Northern) — instant community signal for that regional audience. No Western equivalent. Functions as hook + parasocial anchor simultaneously.
- **Controversy / Tranh cãi hook**: "Bán hàng mà chỉ tập trung làm content viral là sai!" — used for commerce education and opinion content.
- **Tips/value hook**: "Bạn chỉ cần 1 câu duy nhất để khiến khách mua hàng" — high save rate, high share rate.

### Hook layering (multi-modal)

Best practice: simultaneous visual + audio + text overlay attack. Vietnamese educators (Anh Sắc Ánh, EQVN) explicitly state "a single sentence in the first 3 seconds is no longer enough in 2025." Score: single-layer (weak), dual-layer (standard), triple-layer (strong).

### Hook-body contract

Promise made in seconds 0–3 must begin to pay off by seconds 4–10. Violation = retention cliff at second 4.

### First-frame composition

Face vs. no-face (face-in-frame lifts CTR). Face in upper third (Reels UI clears it). Product/subject occupying ≥30% of frame. Text overlay ≤10 words. For Vietnamese commerce: does the first frame show the product outcome, the price, or the creator's reaction?

### Timing of speech / face

Spoken hook should fire by 0.5s; on-screen text overlay should appear by 0.3s. No silent opening on Vietnamese TikTok — Vietnamese viewers consume with sound on at higher rates than Western markets.

## §4. Script & Narrative Structure

### Global information architecture

The canonical arc: hook (0–3s) → problem agitation (4–10s) → demonstration/proof (11–20s) → CTA (final 5–10s). Educational content: tease → setup → reveal → reinforce.

### Vietnamese affiliate formula [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]

Standardized 5-phase structure taught across Vietnamese marketing agencies:
1. **Hook** (0–3s): product outcome or price shock
2. **Product showcase** (3–10s): close-up product reveal, brand/price display
3. **Benefits / Demo** (10–40s): demonstration of the product solving the stated problem
4. **Social proof** (optional): screenshot of orders, testimonials, before/after
5. **CTA** (last 5–10s): verbal + visual — "Bấm vào giỏ hàng vàng," "Link ở bio," discount code in comments

### Livestream funnel script [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]

Video is a trailer for a live session — different structure:
1. Hook with teaser of live deal ("Tối nay mình live, có deal đặc biệt…")
2. Product preview (not full demo — incomplete by design)
3. Urgency CTA to join live ("Vào live với mình để xem giá…")
Evaluate: does the video create genuine curiosity about the live, or does it over-deliver and reduce live attendance?

### Pacing of information release

Reveal value early — don't delay the aha moment. Reset attention every 2–3 seconds with audio, visual, or framing shift. Vietnamese commerce content: demonstrate product benefit within first 15s or lose purchase intent.

### Story types (classify each video)

Problem-Solution, Before/After, Listicle, POV/Storytime, Talking-Head, Tutorial/How-to, Transformation, Unbox/Haul, Myth-bust, Hot-take, Reaction, GRWM, BTS, Price comparison (Vietnamese commerce-specific), Dialect comedy sketch (Vietnamese-specific), Cinematic mini-series episode (Vietnamese-specific).

### Dialogue quality

Hyper-specific pain points beat generic ones. Vietnamese: casual "mình/bạn" direct address outperforms formal language for KOC content. Commerce creators use "chị/em" register for warmth and proximity.

### Credibility signals

Named expert role, demonstrable proof in-frame, results screenshot, "cam kết hàng thật giá thật" (authentic product real price) for commerce. Vietnamese audience is highly skeptical of staged reviews — authenticity signals are critical.

## §5. Editing & Visual Production

- **Cut frequency**: Something visually meaningful every 3–5 seconds. For Vietnamese commerce: product demo segments can sustain longer shots if the product action is visually engaging. For entertainment/comedy: faster (2–3s reset).
- **Transition types**: Hard cut (default), whip pan, match cut, jump cut (deliberate only). CapCut-native transitions (used by ~90% of Vietnamese creators) are algorithmically neutral — use them freely. Over-stylized transitions feel un-native.
- **B-roll ratio**: A-roll:B-roll roughly 60:40 for talking-head. B-roll covers jump cuts. Vietnamese commerce: close-up product shots, unboxing sequences, and result demonstrations are B-roll that doubles as proof.
- **Color grading**: Native, lightly-graded look beats commercial polish. High-key for beauty/skincare (Vietnamese standard). Desaturated/teal for serious/educational.
- **Text overlay design**: CapCut "Classic" and "Proxima Nova" defaults are TikTok-vernacular in Vietnam. 60–80pt for primary text. Kinetic word-by-word for retention. Color-coded for emphasis. Vietnamese: large bold text in frame 1 is standard — not optional.
- **Caption / subtitle styling**: Burned-in or auto-captioned. Vietnamese viewers consume with sound on at higher rates than Western markets, but captions still improve retention — verify readability at 360px width.
- **Safe zone compliance**: Keep all text/CTA overlays out of the bottom 35% (TikTok Shop cart icon) and top 15% (profile area). Vietnamese commerce creators frequently violate this, causing critical CTAs to be obscured.
- **Visual hierarchy**: One dominant element per frame. Eye should land on the subject within 200ms.

## §6. Sound & Audio [SIGNIFICANTLY UPDATED FOR VIETNAM]

Sound is a primary distribution lever in Vietnamese TikTok, not a production afterthought. Vietnamese educators explicitly state that sound-driven hooks outperform controversy hooks for FYP distribution on TikTok Vietnam. Evaluate sound with equal weight to hook analysis.

- **Voice quality**: Direct-mic'd, no room echo, normalized to −14 LUFS, no clipping. Vietnamese creators frequently use CapCut voice enhancement — functional but detectable; authentic voice preferred for KOC content.
- **Speaking pace**: 160–180 WPM for educational content. Vietnamese: conversational pace with natural "à," "ừ," "nha" filler words signals authenticity for KOC persona. Scripted-sounding delivery depresses trust in commerce content.
- **Audio hook timing**: First spoken syllable by 0.3–0.5s. No silent opening. Vietnamese users consume with sound on — the audio hook is as critical as the visual hook.
- **V-pop / Vietnamese remix trending sound [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: Assess sound against TikTok Creative Center's Vietnam-specific trending sound list. Riding an emerging V-pop or Vietnamese remix trend in the first 24–48 hours of its emergence is a documented reach multiplier. Score: is the creator early (emerging), on-time (growth/peak), or late (declining)?
- **Sound trend strategy**: Categorize by lifecycle — Emerging / Growth / Peak / Parody / Decline. Vietnamese TikTok sound trends cycle in 7–14 days. Business accounts cannot use most trending V-pop sounds (Commercial Music Library restriction) — flag this constraint.
- **Music energy alignment**: BPM matched to cut rhythm. Emotional valence matched to content valence. Tempo-synced cuts measurably lift loop rate.
- **Dialect audio [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: Regional dialect in VO is a content category and an engagement signal — not just a style choice. Huế, Quảng Nam, Southern, Northern Vietnamese dialects each carry a distinct audience community. Evaluate: does the dialect match the creator's established persona? Is it consistent? Forced dialect adoption (non-native speaker using a regional accent inauthentically) is a credibility risk.
- **Sound layering**: Background music (−20 dB), VO (−3 dB), SFX punches (−6 dB) on transitions. Vietnamese commerce: satisfying unboxing sounds (packaging, product reveal) function as additional hook layer.
- **Silent-viewing layer**: Burned-in captions mandatory despite higher sound-on rates. Verify legibility.

## §7. Psychological & Emotional Triggers

Drawing on Berger & Milkman's high-arousal-emotion virality findings, mirror-neuron/parasocial research, and dopamine-loop literature — calibrated for Vietnamese cultural context.

- **High-arousal emotions** (drive shares): awe, anger, anxiety, amusement, inspiration. Humor lifts viral probability ~50% (Berger & Milkman). Vietnamese: family-situation humor and relatable daily-life frustration (not abstract concepts) are the dominant high-arousal triggers.
- **Relatability / empathy**: "That's so me" recognition moments. Vietnamese: named daily frustrations in Vietnamese life context ("nỗi khổ của dân văn phòng," student life, motherhood) outperform generic relatability.
- **Social proof / aspiration**: visible order counts, named commission amounts, transformation evidence. Vietnamese commerce: showing actual order numbers ("ra 300 đơn từ 1 video") is a primary trust trigger.
- **Fear / FOMO**: scarcity, "avoid this mistake," missed-opportunity framing. Vietnamese commerce: "flash sale," "chỉ hôm nay," "số lượng có hạn" are culturally normalized urgency signals — not considered manipulative in the Vietnamese commerce context.
- **Curiosity-gap exploitation**: open-loop scripting, cliffhanger before end-screen.
- **Sự thật trần trụi / Raw honesty trigger [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: "Mình đã dùng qua 5 loại, đều thất vọng vì…" (I've tried 5 types and was disappointed because…) — authentic negative experience before positive resolution is a high-trust signal unique to Vietnamese KOC culture. Polished, only-positive reviews are treated with skepticism.
- **Comment-bait design**: polarization without alienation — deliberate controversy framing, opinion-divides, open questions.
- **Share triggers**: identity-expressive content (viewer forwards to "be seen as" the type who shares this), insider info, gift-style value, regional identity content ("người miền Trung sẽ hiểu").
- **Save triggers**: lists, recipes, references, tutorials, "for later" framing. Vietnamese: "lưu lại xem sau" (save to watch later) prompts in commerce content directly signal reference value to the algorithm.

## §8. Trend Signals [UPDATED WITH DOUYIN LAYER]

- **Trend modalities**: sound trends, format/template trends (CapCut templates carry the strongest signal), hashtag trends, visual-style trends, on-screen-text trends, gesture/dance trends, slang/vocabulary trends (Vietnamese slang cycles quarterly — see §11).
- **Trend lifecycle (auditable phases)**: Emergence → Growth → Peak → Parody → Decline. Typical Vietnamese TikTok cycle: 7–14 days for mega-trends. Late entry (post-Peak) is detectable by: broad brand adoption, parody versions, mainstream news coverage.

### Douyin-to-Vietnam pipeline [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]

This is the highest-value trend intelligence signal for Vietnamese TikTok. Vietnamese creators and agencies systematically monitor Douyin (TikTok China) to predict what will trend in Vietnam 2–4 weeks later. No equivalent cross-market forecasting practice exists at scale in Western markets.

Evaluate:
- **Douyin origin detection**: Does this format, sound, visual style, or hook type have a traceable Douyin precedent from 2–6 weeks ago?
- **Vietnam adoption stage**: If Douyin-origin confirmed, is this video at the leading edge (competitive advantage), mid-curve (standard execution), or trailing (too late)?
- **Migration fit**: Does the Douyin format translate cleanly to Vietnamese cultural context, or does it require localization? (Some Douyin trends do not migrate due to cultural specificity)

- **Platform-native vs. imported**: TikTok-origin trends outperform imported trends on TikTok. Reels-native audio outperforms cross-posted TikTok audio. TikTok watermarks are demoted on Reels and Shorts.
- **Trend longevity indicators**: rising saves (intent signal), repeat use by same creator, multi-region adoption, rising mid-tier creator usage.
- **Trend-fit risk**: forced fit, late entry, surface-level copying without community behavior understanding.

## §9. Engagement Architecture [UPDATED FOR VIETNAM]

- **Comment-bait techniques**: open-ended question prompts, deliberate controversy framing, missing-information teases, polarizing opinion statements. Note: Meta's explicit "Comment X below" / "Tag a friend" / "Vote with emoji" is demoted; genuine open questions are not.
- **Comment-as-CTA surface [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: Vietnamese creators systematically use pinned comments as the primary affiliate CTA infrastructure — affiliate links, discount codes, product links, and voucher codes are placed in pinned comments. This is a primary conversion surface, not just an engagement metric. Evaluate: is the pinned comment optimized? Does the video VO reference it ("mã giảm giá trong comment")?
- **Share triggers**: identity-expressive content, group-chat-able moments, regional identity content, in-joke punchlines. Vietnamese: content that names a shared Vietnamese experience (regional, generational, professional) has high group-chat-share potential.
- **Save triggers**: list/reference structure, "lưu lại xem sau" overlay, dense info-graphic frame near end.
- **Duet / Stitch / Remix invitation**: explicit prompts, green-screen format, split-frame layout, hanging questions.
- **CTA type and timing**: Verbal CTA spoken aloud (not just displayed) — documented Vietnamese conversion best practice. CTA in the final 5–10s for entertainment; CTA can be earlier (15–20s) in commerce content where purchase intent is established faster. Front-loaded CTAs on Shorts increase swipe-away.
- **Livestream funnel CTA [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: "Tối nay mình live lúc 8h — vào nhé!" — evaluate: is the livestream time specific? Is there a genuine reason to attend (exclusive deal, limited product)? Vague livestream CTAs convert poorly.
- **Loop architecture**: end-frame that pairs with first-frame creates seamless rewatch — boosts completion percentage and looping rate, both ranking signals on Shorts.

## §10. Platform Compliance & Risk [UPDATED WITH VIETNAM LAW]

- **Restricted-keyword scan (Vietnamese-specific phrases)**: TikTok OCR scans on-screen text AND auto-transcribes audio. Vietnamese restricted phrases that trigger shadowban or distribution throttling:
  - Health claims: "cam kết khỏi hẳn" (guaranteed cure), "trị dứt điểm" (eliminate permanently), "100% hiệu quả," "chữa trị," "trị dứt điểm mụn"
  - Price guarantees: "giá rẻ nhất thị trường," "cam kết hoàn tiền 100%"
  - Platform cross-promotion: "add Zalo," "inbox Facebook," "link YouTube" (directing off-platform)
  - Violence/shock content: any graphic language even in comedy contexts
- **Hashtag risk**: known banned/restricted tags suppress reach. Check: does the in-app search for this tag return a "Learn more about Community Guidelines" notice?
- **Price-anchoring compliance [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: TikTok Shop Vietnam has specific rules on false price anchoring — "giá gốc" (original price) crossed out must reflect a genuine previous price, not an inflated fictional anchor. Flag manipulative price displays.
- **Vietnam Advertising Law 2025 [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: Effective January 1, 2026. KOLs and KOCs must legally disclose commercial relationships. Requirements:
  - Must have used or understand the product before advertising
  - Must display disclosure visibly (not buried in captions)
  - Joint liability for harmful advertising claims
  - Fines: 20–30M VND (~$800–1,200) for non-disclosure
  - High-profile enforcement confirmed — this is actively prosecuted
  - Flag: is the commercial relationship disclosed? Is the disclosure prominent or buried?
- **Copyright risk**: Business accounts must use Commercial Music Library. Hash-fingerprint detects re-uploaded identical files — files must be re-edited.
- **AI-content disclosure**: TikTok/Meta require labeling when footage is AI-generated or materially AI-modified (deepfake face, synthetic avatar, generative backgrounds, obvious TTS). Extraction fields: `ai_generated_suspected`, `ai_disclosure_present`, `ai_disclosure_form` (`platform_label` | `caption_hashtag` | `text_overlay` | `voice` | `none`). Flag when suspected=true and disclosure absent — higher salience on commercial/ads paths.
- **Shadowban diagnostic signals**: ≥50% drop in views overnight; FYP traffic source falling to 0%; unique-hashtag test failing; Account Status flags. Galloway's 3.3B-Short analysis: >52% swipe-away ratio triggers distribution collapse within hours.
- **Competitor-mention risk**: on-screen brand mentions can trigger ad rejection on Meta and audio strikes on TikTok.

## §11. Persona & Brand Consistency [REPLACED JUNGIAN FRAMEWORK WITH VIETNAMESE PERSONAS]

The Jungian brand archetype system (Sage, Hero, Jester, Outlaw, etc.) does not map correctly onto Vietnamese creator personas. Replace with the six Vietnamese creator persona types derived from Vietnamese TikTok market research.

### Six Vietnamese creator personas

| Persona | Vietnamese Term | What It Looks Like | Signature Signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Expert/Authority** | Chuyên gia | Doctor, skincare specialist, finance advisor — credential-first. Authority is stated, not implied. | Professional setting, formal register, statistics-heavy, slow deliberate delivery |

A video using current slang is a freshness signal; using dated slang is a staleness signal. Current high-volume terms (2025–2026): "+1 máy" (expressing agreement, ~3M discussions), "đỉnh khoai" (top-tier/awesome), "toang" (failed/collapsed), "Bớt ảo tưởng đi má" (stop being delusional). YouNet Media Slang Report 2025 documented 72 hot slang terms generating 25M discussions, with TikTok accounting for 70% of all slang origination.

## §12. Performance Prediction

Leading indicators auditors use to forecast outcomes before full distribution. Benchmarks below calibrated for Vietnamese TikTok where Vietnamese-specific data is available; otherwise global benchmarks apply pending Vietnamese corpus calibration (target: ≥500 videos/niche for reliable Vietnamese norms).

- **3-second view rate**: Global ad benchmark — strong: 35–45%, top-quartile: 50–60%, problem: <25% (Stackmatix). Vietnamese corpus target: calibrate per niche after 500+ videos indexed.
- **Shorts "viewed vs. swiped-away" ratio**: Galloway's 3.3B-Short analysis — 70–90% viewed = exploit zone; <48% viewed = distribution death. 75–80% is the operator target.
- **Average percentage viewed**: 65–70%+ indicates hook-body alignment; <60% indicates hook-body contract break.
- **Full-watch / completion rate**: TikTok algorithm's strongest ranking signal beyond 3-second rate. 7–13s videos deliver highest completion in most verticals.
- **Loop rate**: auto-loops on TikTok inflate completion above 100% — strongly correlated with FYP push.
- **Share rate** (sends per impression): predicts cross-network spread. Strongest endogenous virality signal. Vietnamese: group-chat shares (Zalo, Messenger) are not captured in platform analytics — actual share impact is higher than platform-reported.
- **Save rate**: predicts return engagement and signals utility/reference value. For Vietnamese commerce: high save rate on affiliate content indicates the viewer is "saving for purchase later" — a strong lagging conversion indicator.
- **Comment rate and quality**: emotion-prediction models achieve >80% engagement-prediction accuracy using arousal/valence features. Vietnamese: length and specificity of comments (product questions, personal experience shares) are higher-quality signals than emoji comments from tương tác chéo groups.
- **Commerce conversion metrics [VIETNAM-SPECIFIC]**: For affiliate/shop videos, the performance stack extends beyond engagement:
  - Click-through to cart (giỏ hàng vàng / cam click rate)
  - Add-to-cart rate
  - Order completion rate ("ra đơn")
  - Commission per video ("hoa hồng/video")
  - A video with 80K views and 300 orders outperforms a video with 2M views and 0 orders in Vietnamese commerce context.
- **Niche benchmark context**: always score relative to vertical decile, not absolute thresholds. A 40% completion rate is failing in dance/comedy but elite in long-form educational SFV.
- **Creator velocity context**: account averaging 50K views per video that hits 200K in hour one signals exploit-phase entry; same number from a 2M-average account signals decline.
- **NAWP / ECR**: Kuaishou's Normalized Average Watch Percentage and Engagement Continuation Rate — the closest research proxies for the algorithm's internal ranking.
