# The Content Research Process Behind "What Should I Film Today?" — A Practitioner-Level Breakdown for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts

## TL;DR
- The serious answer to "what should I film today?" is almost never one decision — it's the output of a layered weekly research loop that combines TikTok-native signals (Creative Center, Creator Search Insights, FYP), competitor and sales-data tools (Kalodata, Fastmoss, Shoplus, Exolyt, Pentos, Sprout Social, Brand24), and a creator's own pre-defined content pillars; the output of that loop is a shortlist of hook + format + sound combinations that get batched and tested.
- Professional agencies (VaynerMedia, Whalar, Jungle Creations) and Vietnamese MCNs (UpBase, Melive/Metub, Vulaci, Adpia) differentiate from solo creators not by knowing trends faster but by industrialising the loop — proprietary insight stacks (Jungle iQ and Pollinate, Whalar's Foam, VaynerMedia's PAC/SOC/VVM offerings), studio infrastructure, brief templates, post-mortem data reviews, and KOC training studios that translate signals into shootable scripts within hours.
- The decision logic is convergence: a creator films "X with hook Y and sound Z today" when (a) a search-demand gap or rising sound shows up in Creative Center / Creator Search Insights, (b) the format is already winning in their niche on competitor accounts and Top Ads, (c) it maps to a pre-existing content pillar, and (d) their own analytics show that format type drives completion and saves above their threshold — usually 70%+ completion in 2026.

## Key Findings

1. **There is a standard 5-stage weekly workflow used across professional shops.** It runs Monday→Friday and folds into a continuous daily 10–20 minute trend scan plus a weekly briefing session. Stages: (1) signal scan, (2) competitor and Top-Ads audit, (3) hook/format extraction, (4) brief and batch-record, (5) post-mortem.

2. **TikTok-native tools are the spine of the research stack.** TikTok Creative Center (Trend Discovery → Hashtags/Songs/Creators/Videos, Top Ads, Keyword Insights, Symphony Assistant) and the in-app Creator Search Insights panel are used as daily intake. Creator Search Insights has become "the closest thing short-form video has to a free keyword planner," with creators filtering by their niche and sorting by **content gap** — high demand, low supply queries — to pick three to five shootable queries per week.

3. **Third-party tools split into four functional buckets**, and serious shops layer them: (a) shop/sales intelligence — Kalodata, Fastmoss, Shoplus, PiPiAds (Kalodata offers ~500 days of historical TikTok Shop data; Fastmoss tracks 500M+ products with 28-day product-level analytics, with 1,200-day historical data gated to its $139/month Ultimate plan — Basic is $49/month for 90-day data, Pro is $89/month for 180-day; Shoplus does hourly updates); (b) TikTok-only analytics/social listening — Exolyt, Pentos, Brand24, Keyhole; (c) cross-platform listening — Sprout Social, Brandwatch, Talkwalker, Meltwater, Sprinklr (each added TikTok hashtag/video listening in 2024–2025); (d) influencer and competitor discovery — HypeAuditor, Socialinsider, Storyclash, Iconosquare. Pentos is explicitly tracker-driven — "no historical numbers can be pulled in" until a tracker is placed, which forces shops to set up tracking on competitors weeks before a campaign.

4. **The Vietnamese market has its own native stack.** Buzzmetrics' "Trend Spotter" is the published standard alongside YouNet Media's SocialHeat (3 years of historical data, AI-enhanced dashboard launched 2025). Kompa runs an explicit 3-layer TikTok analysis framework (Content & Trend Analysis → User Behavior & Engagement Signals → Customer Journey Mapping) for clients including Vinamilk, Shopee and Vietjet. Hiip.asia (founded 2016, 3M+ influencers, in-house ML/NLP/image recognition platform), 7SAT/7Saturday (20,000+ influencer profiles, MOG-owned), Revu.vn (155,000 influencers, 5,000+ campaigns), and AnyTag (AnyMind) handle KOL/KOC discovery and seeding.

5. **Vietnamese TikTok MCN/TSPs have documented, repeatable workflows.** UpBase MCN publishes a 5-step booking flow (plan → KOC list → contract → QC → acceptance report) and an internal **"Win Key Content" testing methodology** — continuously testing short-video formats to find a repeatable winner that they then scale via TikTok Ads. Melive Network (subsidiary of Metub Network, first official TikTok Shop MCN in Vietnam) operates **10 in-house livestream studios** and runs a post-live data analysis session by its operations crew after every stream. Adpia MCN runs a **1-month / 3-month / 6-month creator roadmap** with monthly trend workshops, weekly platform-policy updates, daily algo/edit tips pushed to creators, plus content kits and video edit kits that pre-build the first-second hook. Vulaci Max (Vulaci Network's TikTok Shop arm, official since April 2022) explicitly focuses on **"GMV growth and livestream process consulting"** and "closely tracks and updates the latest trends on TikTok Shop Vietnam."

6. **The information collected falls into five buckets that map to the algorithm.** (i) Trend signals: hook types, format archetypes, sounds (organic vs Commercial Music Library — they are different libraries; mixing them in paid ads has caused brand takedown notices), hashtag velocity (7-day vs 30-day movement), creators rising. (ii) Competitor intelligence: top-performing ads in vertical, share-of-voice, pattern rate of hook types in top 20. (iii) Platform signals: completion-rate cliff at ~85%, the 70% completion threshold reported as the 2026 "viral floor", view-velocity 30-minute window for tier classification, follower-first testing in late-2025/2026 (videos now seeded to followers first). Per Hootsuite's 2025 Social Trends Report, as cited by Greenfroglabs' TikTok Algorithm 2026 guide, "TikTok videos with a save rate above 2% were 3.4x more likely to appear on the For You page than videos with similar view counts but lower save rates." (iv) Audience signals: comments-to-saves ratio, profile-visit rate (newer 2026 signal weighted ~2.5× a like by third-party researchers), rewatch/loop rate. (v) Commerce signals (TikTok Shop specifically): GMV trends by SKU, commission rate, top-creator-of-product to reverse-engineer hooks; standard filter is "Top Products → commission ≥10% → 7-day growth → list of selling creators."

7. **Decision logic — convergence beats single-signal chasing.** A creator films "X with hook Y and sound Z today" when at least three of the following overlap: a Creator Search Insights gap matches their niche; the sound is in Creative Center Trend Discovery's organic (non-commercial) breakout list before it hits Billboard/Spotify viral (a practitioner-cited 3–5 day window); the format already appears in five or more Top Ads in their vertical at the 30-day window; the hook archetype is one of the proven 5–6 patterns (Pattern Interrupt, Curiosity Gap, Bold Claim, Storytelling, Visual/Sensory, Proof-First); and the topic fits one of their 3–5 pre-set content pillars under a 70/20/10 split (70% value, 20% community, 10% promotion). Agencies add a sixth gate: brand-fit/legal/compliance check.

8. **Pain points cluster around four breakage points.** (a) Trend timing — by the time a sound is on a "weekly trends" blog list, it's already past optimal entry; the early pre-spotting window is hard to systematize. (b) Hook-content mismatch — clickbait hooks that don't deliver tank through-play and burn audience trust. (c) Ideation fatigue and burnout — Billion Dollar Boy's July 2025 Censuswide survey of 1,000 US and UK creators found: "More than half (52%) of creators have experienced burnout as a direct result of their career, leading nearly two in five (37%) to actively consider leaving the profession altogether." TikTok comedian Brad Gosse summarised the symptom: "I try to come up with ideas and I come up blank." (d) Commercial Music Library confusion — organic trending sounds are not cleared for paid ads, and "mixing the two has cost brands takedown notices."

9. **The agency vs solo creator gap is industrial, not informational.** Solo creators have access to almost the same free tools (Creative Center, Creator Search Insights, native analytics, Brand24/Exolyt freemium tiers). Agencies' actual edge is: proprietary measurement stacks (Whalar partners with Kantar's LINK AI to score creative effectiveness pre-launch; Jungle Creations' Jungle iQ and Pollinate; VaynerMedia's PAC/SOC/VVM frameworks); physical studio infrastructure (Whalar's Lighthouse campus, Melive's 10 studios); approval and brief workflows (Sprout Social, Planable, Notion templates) that let three to five reviewers turn around a brief in hours; performance-creative loops that produce 12+ variants per concept; and the Kantar/Nielsen-style measurement that lets agencies justify volume to brand finance teams.

## Details

### The weekly workflow, stage by stage

**Stage 1 — Daily signal scan (10–20 minutes, every weekday morning).** Open the FYP and scroll for 10 minutes with the brand/niche lens on. Note recurring sounds, formats, and visual motifs that appear three or more times across different creators — that's the early signal that a format is generalising, not a one-off. Cross-reference what you see in the wild against Creative Center → Trend Discovery (Hashtags, Songs, Creators, Videos), set to your target region and the 7-day window. Filter organic (non-commercial) sounds first because they're earlier in the lifecycle; commercial-library tracks are already mainstream. The AdMapix-documented agency cadence runs Monday FYP scroll → Tuesday Top Ads log (5 rows: advertiser, hook, CTA, format, duration) → Wednesday competitor deep-dive (3 advertisers: 2 direct competitors plus 1 adjacent-category winner) → Thursday hook pattern classification → Friday brief.

**Stage 2 — Weekly Creative Center / Creator Search Insights audit (~30–60 minutes, typically Monday or Tuesday).** This is the search-demand layer. In the Creator Search Insights panel in-app, filter by niche, sort by **content gap**, and pick three to five queries that you can film this week without new gear. For each chosen query, the literal query becomes the on-screen text in the first second, the spoken hook in the first three seconds, and at least one line of the caption — TikTok's search ranker leans heavily on text-on-screen plus captions. In Creative Center, screenshot the first three seconds of the top 20 ads in your vertical (set the 2025 sub-vertical filter, 30-day window, sorted by 6-second view rate). That's your "hook library" for the week. Pattern rate (how often a hook type appears in the top 20) is the strongest signal — stronger than any single ad.

**Stage 3 — Hook and format extraction.** The serious shops don't copy the ad — they extract the structural framework: hook mechanism (first 3 seconds), narrative arc, cuts-per-second, audio style, CTA placement, and on-screen text density. Hook archetypes that consistently appear at the top: Pattern Interrupt, Curiosity Gap, Bold Statement, Direct Call-out ("Stop scrolling"), Storytelling Cliffhanger, Visual/Sensory (drop, ASMR, satisfying reveal), Proof-First (show the result, then explain). TikTok for Business reports that 63% of videos with the highest click-through rate hook their audience within the first three seconds. Retention research converges on the 85% completion cliff — videos hitting at least 85% completion get a roughly 4× distribution multiplier; the 70% completion threshold has emerged as the "viral floor" in 2026 reporting.

**Stage 4 — Brief, batch, record.** Solo creators write a one-page brief per video: hook line, sound, on-screen text, b-roll list, payoff/CTA, target pillar. Agencies use Sprout Social or Planable approval workflows where a social media manager drafts, a creative director reviews, and a brand lead approves. Then batch — record 3–5 (or 5–12 for an agency) videos in one session. Buffer-reported industry guidance puts the ROI sweet spot at 3–5 posts per week; batching saves an estimated 60% of production time. Editing day is separate. Trending audio is usually layered on inside TikTok at upload time, not in CapCut, so creators can swap audio if a sound dies between editing and posting.

**Stage 5 — Post-mortem (mid-week and end-of-week).** Pull TikTok Analytics: 24-hour read (watch time, completion rate, shares per 1,000 views, traffic source), 7-day read (decide to remake, extend into a series, or retire the format). Melive Network publicly states that "after each livestream, the Melive MCN team analyzes real data to give improvement and optimization recommendations" — the same loop applied to short-form. Top 20% of content by completion rate gets analysed for shared format/length/hook/topic; according to TikTok's Creator Learning Hub (updated January 2026), accounts that identify their top-performing content type within 30 days and increase production of that type by 50%+ see an average 3× improvement in reach over the following 60 days.

**TikTok-native (free, used daily).**
- *Creative Center → Trend Discovery* — the primary briefing input for sounds and hashtags. Used at the weekly scan stage. Set region, filter by industry, compare 7-day vs 30-day to distinguish spikes from durable shifts. Songs tab has a "Breakout" filter for newly rising audio.
- *Top Ads in Creative Center* — used to extract winning hook patterns and pacing benchmarks; sorted by 6-second view rate or CTR by industry.
- *Keyword Insights* — used to populate captions and on-screen text with high-search-volume terms; a TikTok Business Account unlocks list saving/export.
- *Creator Search Insights (in-app)* — the search-demand panel; treat it like a shopping list, not a brainstorm. Sort by content gap, not raw demand.
- *Creator Academy + TikTok Business Newsletter* — used for algorithm/policy updates.
- *Symphony Assistant (added 2025)* — AI script generator and creative-studio inside Creative Center; used by agencies to draft 8–12 hook variants per brief in minutes.

**Third-party — Shop intelligence (TikTok Shop sellers and Vietnamese MCNs).**

Practitioner recipe used in Vietnam: Top Products → Commission Rate ≥10% → last-7-day filter → list of best affiliate products → look at the creator list selling them → study video format/caption/link placement. ### Decision logic — converting signals into "I'm filming X today"

The collected signals translate into a content decision through a layered AND-gate, not a single signal trigger. The practitioner mental model:

1. **Pillar fit** — does the topic sit inside one of the creator's 3–5 pre-set content pillars? If no, kill it. Pillars are the strategic constant; trends are the tactical variable.
2. **Search-demand evidence** — is there a Creator Search Insights query with high demand and a visible content gap? If yes, the query becomes the on-screen text and the spoken hook in seconds 0–3.
3. **Format evidence** — is the format already winning in this niche? Two checks: (a) the format appears in 5+ of the Top 20 Ads in vertical in the last 30 days, and (b) the format has a "pattern rate" ≥25% in the FYP scroll.
4. **Sound evidence** — is there a Creative Center breakout sound (organic, not commercial-music-library) rising in the 7-day window but not yet on Billboard or Spotify viral charts? If yes, the practitioner-cited 3–5 day pre-spotting window applies.
5. **Hook archetype mapping** — pick one of the proven 5–6 hook patterns. Write three hook variants (Shock / Mystery / Authority) and pick the one that "feels impossible to ignore."
6. **Performance threshold check** — does the creator's own analytics show this format/topic combination historically clears their internal completion-rate threshold? In 2026 the target is 70%+; top accounts aim for 85%+.

If four of the six gates fire, the video gets filmed today. If only two, it goes into the swipe file for later. The pillar gate is non-negotiable; the others can be partially substituted.

### The Vietnamese TikTok Shop layer

Vietnam is the most TikTok-saturated influencer market in APAC (62.9% of all 2025 influencer campaigns per AnyMind), and the content-decision process is heavily commerce-driven. The practitioner workflow for a TikTok Shop KOC differs from a "pure" content creator in three ways.

First, **product selection precedes content decision**. The KOC opens Kalodata (or Fastmoss/Shoplus), filters Top Products → Commission ≥10% → 7-day GMV growth, gets a shortlist of 5–10 SKUs, then looks at the creator list selling each product to reverse-engineer what video formats are converting. Hằng Du Mục, one of Vietnam's top livestream creators, takes this further — she physically travels to suppliers (Đắk Lắk province for agricultural products, Tân Cương/Xinjiang for tea) to film source-of-product content. In her own words on the methodology (Kenh14, "Nghề Chủ Chốt" interview): "Mountains, landslides — I can still go. Things like this are nothing. I want to share the real story with everyone, want everyone to see that products Hằng Du Mục sells are products I've personally touched, experienced, and verified the quality of." She also describes the creative iteration explicitly: "After many days with no viewers, I self-concluded I was lacking creativity. That's why you see me appearing in livestreams with different settings — outside by the sea, in the mountains, in the snow, in the rain — to create a signature."

Second, **livestream prep is measured in weeks, not days**. Phạm Thoại, one of the most-watched livestreamers in Vietnam, is explicit in VnExpress: "To prepare a quality livestream session, Thoại and his crew need MONTHS. The work isn't just holding the product and stating the price — it includes ideation, scriptwriting, and adjusting to match trends." Vietnamese outlets Dân Trí, Kenh14 and Znews.vn confirm he self-disclosed 50 billion VND (~$2M USD) in GMV in April 2024; a separate Mega Live on April 4 reportedly hit 58 billion VND, with Phạm Thoại publicly stating his 10% commission earned him 5.8 billion VND from that single session. Melive Network's Head, Như Quỳnh, has stated publicly that top KOCs driving 10–20 billion VND per session can collect ~500 million VND in booking fees split across the brands featured.

Third, **MCNs supply the "what to film" briefing infrastructure**. UpBase MCN's published 5-step booking flow runs Plan → KOC shortlist → Contact → QC → Acceptance report. Their internal "Win Key Content" testing process iterates short-video formats to find a repeatable winning concept (an example UpBase has cited: "pretty female reviewing product directly, product shot clearly, couple gifts" became the win-key format that they then scaled with TikTok Ads). Adpia's training studio runs 1-month/3-month/6-month roadmaps for creators with explicit GMV targets, monthly trend workshops, weekly platform-policy updates, and pre-built "content kits" and "video edit kits" that include first-second hook templates. Melive's 10 in-house livestream studios mean creators don't have to source production — they walk in, livestream, and the ops team handles tech, choreography, and a post-live data review session. Vulaci Max (Vulaci's TikTok Shop arm, official partner since April 2022) explicitly "focuses on advising creators on GMV growth strategy as well as the livestream process so that creator content reaches needs and accelerates conversion."

### Pain points where the process breaks down

- **Trend timing is the hardest problem.** The practitioner-cited 3–5 day window between a sound appearing in Creative Center → Trend Discovery (organic) and breaking into the Billboard/Spotify viral charts is the sweet spot. Miss it on the early side and the sound is too obscure to drive distribution; miss it on the late side and you're filming saturated content. Weekly trend digests from agency blogs (Later, New Engen, Ramdam) are almost always trailing indicators by the time they publish.
- **Hook–content mismatch tanks through-play.** A common reported failure mode: a clickbait hook that promises more than the video delivers; engagement spikes in second 3, then collapses by second 8. The fix is "the best hooks are ones where the hook is the content."
- **Commercial Music Library vs organic sounds.** Organic trending sounds are not cleared for paid ads. Brands that boost organic creator content into paid have triggered takedown notices. The professional shops keep two parallel sound shortlists.
- **Ideation fatigue and burnout.** Billion Dollar Boy's July 2025 Censuswide survey of 1,000 US and UK creators reports that "more than half (52%) of creators have experienced burnout as a direct result of their career, leading nearly two in five (37%) to actively consider leaving the profession altogether." Bloomberg's Alex Barinka, in her June 29, 2023 newsletter "TikTok's Fickle Algorithm Is Leading to Burnout for Content Creators," noted that "the same algorithmic force that helps random people go viral on TikTok is also making it tough for career creators to build sustainable businesses." Pillar systems and swipe files exist precisely to remove the daily creative decision.
- **Vanity-metric chasing.** Likes are the weakest 2026 signal; saves and shares now carry more weight, but creators still optimise for what's most visible (like count). Save rate above 2% is the meaningful threshold for FYP boost per Hootsuite's 2025 Social Trends Report.
- **Pentos "tracker-first" gotcha.** A first-time agency user assumes Pentos has historical TikTok data; it does not, until trackers are placed. This wastes one to two weeks before a campaign.
- **Algorithm churn from the US TikTok divestiture (January 2026).** The retraining of the US algorithm on American data via the Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX deal has caused distribution fluctuations during the transition; agencies are advising clients to "focus on the fundamentals — watch time, completion rate, shares" rather than chase short-term ranking shifts.

## Recommendations

**If you are a solo creator or small TikTok Shop seller (Vietnam or elsewhere):**
1. Define 3–5 content pillars under a 70/20/10 split (value/community/promo). Review them every 60–90 days against your analytics.
2. Run a 20-minute daily FYP scan logged in a Notion or Airtable swipe file (hook line, sound, format, what made you stop scrolling). Pattern rate is more important than any single video.
3. Once a week (30 minutes), open Creative Center → Trend Discovery (region = your market, 7-day window) and Creator Search Insights (sorted by content gap). Pick 3–5 queries for the week. Write the query as your first-line text/hook.
4. Once a week (30 minutes), audit the top 20 ads in your vertical in Top Ads. Screenshot the first 3 seconds. Build a hook library.
5. Batch record 3–5 videos in one session; edit and schedule across the week. Layer trending audio at upload (not in edit) so you can swap if it dies.
6. Set thresholds — 70% completion is your floor for the format being worth repeating, save rate above 2% is the meaningful FYP boost signal per Hootsuite's 2025 Social Trends Report. Below threshold for two consecutive videos, retire the format.
7. For TikTok Shop: subscribe to Kalodata or Fastmoss (~$40–$55/month at entry tiers) and run the standard recipe weekly — Top Products → commission ≥10% → 7-day growth → study top selling creators' videos.

**If you are a brand or agency running TikTok at scale:**
1. Adopt the AdMapix-style 5-stage weekly cadence (Mon FYP → Tue Top Ads log → Wed competitor deep-dive → Thu hook classification → Fri brief). Make it a documented SOP, not tribal knowledge.
2. License or build a pre-launch creative effectiveness layer (Kantar LINK AI, Whalar's Foam, or a similar custom benchmark) so you can score 8–12 hook variants before producing. The cost of generating variants in 2025–2026 has collapsed; the bottleneck is decision-making.
3. Build a Creator Search Insights + Keyword Insights pipeline that maps to your owned content pillars per client — this is the single biggest "underpriced attention" opportunity in 2026 because most agencies still treat short-form like paid TV.
4. Run a post-each-piece data review (Melive's livestream-debrief model applied to short-form): top 20% by completion rate analysed for shared format, length, hook, topic; production volume of that type increases by 50%+ over the next 30 days.
5. In Vietnam specifically: pair a Kalodata/Fastmoss product-research seat with a Buzzmetrics or YouNet SocialHeat subscription for sentiment, plus an MCN partnership (UpBase, Melive, Vulaci, or Adpia) for KOC sourcing and livestream production.

**Thresholds that should change your strategy:**
- Completion rate < 50% on three videos in a row → kill the format.
- Save rate < 1% → the content isn't useful enough; rework or retire.
- Cost per qualified view stuck above $0.05 (paid) → your hook is the problem, not the targeting; rebuild the first 3 seconds.
- For Vietnam TikTok Shop creators: GMV per livestream falling for 3 consecutive sessions → audit product selection in Kalodata, not livestream technique.

## Caveats

- **Many of the most specific data points come from agency-published or tool-published material**, which is inherently self-promotional. Kalodata's own ToS warns its numbers are directional, not source-of-truth. Whalar's Nielsen ROI numbers come from Nielsen-conducted studies commissioned and cited by Whalar; the figures vary between studies ($2.63 ROAS average in the six-campaign measurement; $2.43 in the MMM study; the often-quoted "300% of impact with less than 1% of media" line is a Whalar-cited Nielsen finding rather than an independent benchmark).
- **The "completion rate cliff at 85%", "view velocity 30-minute window", and "saves weighted ~2.5×" figures** are reported by third-party algorithm researchers (Stackmatix, SocialBoostDigital, Greenfroglabs, Sprout Social citing Hootsuite); TikTok has not formally published these weights, so treat them as best-available estimates, not platform-confirmed truth.
- **TikTok algorithm changes in 2025–2026** include the follower-first testing update (videos now shown to existing followers first before non-followers) and the US algorithm retraining under the Oracle deal — both may shift the relative weight of signals between when this report was prepared and when a reader applies it.
- **Vietnamese MCN process descriptions are largely self-published** (UpBase, Adpia, Vulaci blogs). The independent practitioner quotes — Hằng Du Mục, Phạm Thoại, Võ Hà Linh, Melive's Như Quỳnh — come from Kenh14, VnExpress, Cafef, Dân Trí, Znews and the "Nghề Chủ Chốt" TikTok Vietnam-produced creator show, which are stronger sources.
- **Symphony Assistant, Creator Search Insights, and Creative Center sub-vertical filters** are continually updated by TikTok; the specific UI paths described here reflect the 2025–2026 state and will drift.
- **Trend timing claims** ("3–5 day window before Billboard/Spotify viral") are practitioner heuristics from agency blogs, not measured findings.
- **Burnout statistics** — Billion Dollar Boy's July 2025 Censuswide survey (1,000 US/UK creators) is the strongest recent prevalence figure at 52%; older "90%+ of creators report burnout" numbers cited in industry pieces should be treated as directional indicators of a real problem rather than a precise prevalence.
- **7SAT, UpTik, and MCV** are repeatedly listed as official TikTok TSP/MCN partners in Vietnam but have noticeably thinner public practitioner-level documentation than UpBase, Melive, Vulaci, and Adpia. Their internal workflows likely mirror the latter group but are not publicly verified at the same depth.
