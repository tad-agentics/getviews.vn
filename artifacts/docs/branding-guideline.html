01 · The Mark

A G with something to say.

The Getviews mark is a chunky filled G with a tail that doubles as a play arrow. The G is the platform — a window, an eye, a viewport. The tail is what comes out of it: a comment, a video posted, a view counted. Friendly geometry, single solid fill, no gradients, no outlines. The whole thing is one closed path.

  • 1The G · solid ring — a chunky filled G with rounded terminals, weighted slightly heavier on the lower-left curve so the glyph sits visually grounded. Friendly, not corporate.
  • 2The counter · the eye — the inner white space is wider than a typeset G's would be; it reads as a viewport, a window into the feed. This is the room where views happen.
  • 3The crossbar · pulling inward — the horizontal arm stops short of the ring's edge, leaving a gap. That tension turns a static G into something that wants to point.
  • 4The tail · play, posted — the bottom-right notch reads two ways: a chat-bubble tail (the platform talks back) and a play triangle (the moment publish happens). Two reads, one shape.

03 · Rules

Don't squeeze the loop.

The mark needs space to breathe. The G stays solid. The tail stays where it lands — bottom-right, at the play angle. Single fill, never split into two colors. We're playful, not chaotic.

Clearspace = X, where X is the height of the tail notch
Do
Use the mark as drawn — magenta triangle, ink ring, white knockout.
Don't
Never apply gradients, rainbows, or "fun" color treatments to the mark.
Do
On Magenta, switch the whole mark to canvas white. Same drawing, inverted ink.
Don't
Never rotate, skew, stretch, or distort the proportions of the mark.
Do
On dark surfaces, switch the ring + knockout to canvas white.
Don't
Never place the mark on busy textures, photos, or low-contrast backgrounds.

Two accents & a quiet room.

Origin · why these two hues

Magenta — the moment of action

Hotter, deeper, more saturated than the platform pinks creators see all day. Magenta sits closer to fuchsia than coral — it reads as an editor's mark, not a feed's reaction emoji. We use it once per screen: the live dot, the call-to-action, the one number that matters.

HSL 327° · 93% · 56%. Tested at 60% scale on white, ink, and dark canvas — holds at every size.

Sky — the voice of the system

A clean, daytime cyan. We use it for AI output, system feedback, and live data — places where the product itself is talking, not the user. Reading-friendly at small sizes, optimistic without being saccharine.

HSL 196° · 84% · 62%. Reserved for sky chips, AI badges, sparklines, and the "Beta" app icon.

Pairing rules
  • Use Instrument Serif italic once per surface — for one emotional word inside a headline. Never for body. Never for UI.
  • Mono is for metadata, IDs, tags, kicker labels. Always uppercase. Always 0.12em tracking.
  • Never use more than 3 weights per page (e.g. 400 · 500 · 600).
  • Numbers always use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums in tables and big stats.
06 · Iconography

Drafted, not stamped.

Every Getviews icon is a tiny diagram of motion — a spike, a window, a hook, a loop. 1.6px stroke, 24px viewport, square caps, miter joins, pure monochrome. Color is the design system's job, never the icon's.

08 · Voice & Tone

Talk like a creator who's been there.

Direct. Concrete. Slightly dry. We don't yell, we don't use the word "amazing," and we never explain TikTok to TikTok creators. Vietnamese first, then English — same tone in both.

Attribute 01

Direct

Lead with the point. No "we're excited to announce." Just announce it.

Attribute 02

Concrete

Numbers, names, examples. Not "many creators" — "2,148 creators in HCMC."

Attribute 03

Dry-funny

A small wink. Never a punchline. We're a tool, not a comedian.

Attribute 04

Insider

We say "duet," "lipsync," "POV," "hook." We don't explain them.

● Yes · Empty state
Chưa có video nào. Hook đầu tiên đăng tối nay là được.
Acknowledges the state, gives one concrete next step. No exclamation marks.
● No · Empty state
🚀 Welcome aboard, creator! Your epic journey starts here! ✨
Vague, hype-y, three emojis. Sounds like every other onboarding screen.
● Yes · Trend insight
"Đặt tay lên cằm" tăng 312% trong 7 ngày. Beauty + GenZ. Còn nóng ~5 ngày.
Specific number, audience, expiration. The whole point of Getviews in one line.
● No · Trend insight
A new trend is going viral! 🔥 Don't miss out — capitalize now before it's too late!
Vague, urgent, no useful detail. Marketing slop, not insight.
● Yes · Error
Không kết nối được TikTok. Có thể app mới đổi token — thử login lại.
Names the problem, gives a likely cause, gives the action.
● No · Error
Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later.
Tells the user nothing they didn't already know. No path forward.
